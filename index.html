About

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Stanford University, in the Center for Sustainable Development and Global Competitiveness, working with Prof. Jie Wang and Prof. Michael Lepech. I was previously an Associate Research Scientist at Columbia University, and I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Virginia in 2024, advised by Prof. Bradford Campbell. My research aims at translational AI systems: systems that hold up in hospitals, buildings and power grids, financial markets, and the platforms people use every day. Three things have to be true before that is possible, and each is one of my research lines:

  • Trustworthy generation and agents: generation and agents whose output can be checked against the structure of its target.
  • Alignment at large scale: representation alignment and calibration for learning heterogeneous data in one model.
  • Privacy-preserving and efficient learning: federated learning and efficient inference where data cannot leave the device and compute is scarce.
  • Translational AI systems: where the three are put to the test with clinicians, building engineers, developers, and platform teams: clinical reports and sensing in hospitals, buildings and grids, financial markets and open source, generated software, and recommendation platforms serving hundreds of millions of people.

My work appears at ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, AAAI, KDD, WWW, SIGIR, ACM MM and CVPR, in the ACL family, and at systems venues including ICCPS, IPDPS, SenSys and IPSN, and it has been recognized by a Google HE Faculty AI Fellowship, a Best Paper Nomination at the EMNLP Industry Track, orals at ACM MM and CHIL, a Best Paper Candidate at ACM BuildSys, and the World's Top 2% Scientists list. I also contribute to the FinRL open-source ecosystem and organize the FinRL Contests.

Research overview

Trustworthy Generation & Agents

Output that can be checked. Mine the structure of the target first, generate inside it, then verify and repair against that structure, with evaluators built where nothing can be executed.

e.g. Edge medical AI (EMNLP Industry'25) Best Paper Nom., DCM (ICML'26).

ICML ×2 · ICLR · AAAI ×2 · WWW · ACL ×3 · EMNLP ×4 · EACL ×2 · KDD, etc.

Alignment at Large Scale

Several kinds of data learned in one model. Modalities, sub-populations and label distributions have to be aligned and calibrated without one source erasing another, and the methods have to hold at production scale.

e.g. Prototype-guided projection (ACM MM'25) Oral, AGM (NeurIPS'25).

NeurIPS · SIGIR · KDD ×2 · ICML · ACM MM ×3 · CVPR, etc.

Privacy-Preserving & Efficient Learning

Learning where the data and the compute actually are. Data stays where it is collected, budgets are finite, and the accuracy cost of privacy and the energy cost of adaptation are part of the design.

e.g. H-FedSN (NPJ AI'26), LLM economic viability (ACL Industry'26).

NPJ AI · ICCPS · ACL · EMNLP · IPDPS · IEEE TSC, etc.
